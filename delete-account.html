Delete Your LEPT Reviewer Account

LEPT Reviewer provides users with a way to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

To request account deletion:
Send an email to padalapatmc@gmail.com using the email address associated with your LEPT Reviewer account.

What will be deleted?

When your account deletion request is completed, LEPT Reviewer will delete your account and associated user data stored in our application database, including:

  • Your account and authentication record
  • Your profile and account settings
  • Your quiz and practice history
  • Your daily question usage records
  • Your notifications
  • Your chat conversations and chat messages
  • Your payment request records
  • Your subscription records

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to padalapatmc@gmail.com.
  2. Send the request from the email address associated with your LEPT Reviewer account.
  3. Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject of your email.
Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once the deletion is completed, your account and associated application data cannot be restored.

Deletion through the app

Signed-in users can also delete their account directly from the Account section of the LEPT Reviewer app by selecting Delete Account.

Contact
