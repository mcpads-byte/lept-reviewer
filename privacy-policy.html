LEPT Reviewer Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

LEPT Reviewer ("we", "our", or "the app") is an educational mobile application designed to help users prepare for the Licensure Examination for Professional Teachers (LEPT).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and the choices available to you when you use LEPT Reviewer.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account information such as your email address and name.
  • Profile and account settings.
  • Quiz activity, scores, progress, and study history.
  • Questions, answers, bookmarks, and other study-related activity.
  • Subscription and plan information.
  • Payment request information and payment proof submitted by the user.
  • Messages sent through the in-app chat feature.
  • Notification preferences and notification status.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide quizzes, mock exams, and study features.
  • Save your progress and study history.
  • Manage free and premium access.
  • Process subscription and payment requests.
  • Provide customer support through in-app messaging.
  • Send relevant notifications when enabled.
  • Maintain and improve the app.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the service.

3. Account Information

An account is required for features that need your identity, such as saving progress, managing subscriptions, communicating with support, and accessing personalized study information.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure.

4. Quiz and Study Data

LEPT Reviewer may store information about your study activity, including quiz attempts, scores, progress, and related learning activity. This information is used to provide personalized progress and study features.

5. Subscriptions and Payment Requests

The app may provide free and paid subscription plans. If you submit a payment request, information associated with that request may be stored so that the request can be reviewed and your subscription can be managed.

LEPT Reviewer does not intentionally collect your complete bank account, credit card, or other financial account credentials through the app. Users should only submit payment information requested by the app's designated payment process.

6. Chat and Communications

If you use the in-app chat feature, messages you send may be stored and accessed by authorized administrators for customer support and service communication.

7. Notifications

The app may provide notifications related to daily challenges, study reminders, announcements, account activity, and other service-related information. You can manage available notification preferences within the app.

8. Third-Party Services

LEPT Reviewer uses third-party services to provide parts of its functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Our backend and database services are provided through Supabase. Supabase may process account and application data necessary to provide authentication, database, and related backend services.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app's services, maintain account records, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

11. Account Deletion

LEPT Reviewer provides an account deletion function that allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

When an account is deleted, associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible, subject to information that may need to be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or administrative purposes.

12. Children's Privacy

LEPT Reviewer is intended as an educational review application. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

13. Your Choices

Depending on the features available in the app, you may be able to access, update, or delete certain account information and manage notification preferences.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app, our practices, or applicable requirements. When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
